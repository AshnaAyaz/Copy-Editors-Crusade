# Documentation Audit – OpenStreetMap Wiki

This document records the problems we found in the original OpenStreetMap Wiki pages before rewriting them.

> **How to fill this in:** Each writer completes the section for their own topics. Save "before" screenshots in `images/before/` and link them here. Record only what you actually observed on the page.

## Audit criteria

We checked every original page against these criteria:

| # | Criterion | Question we asked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audience fit | Is it written for beginners, or does it assume expert knowledge? |
| 2 | Topic type separation | Are concepts, steps, and reference information mixed together? |
| 3 | Structure | Is the page logically organized? Are headings clear? |
| 4 | Task clarity | Are steps numbered, complete, in order, and one action per step? |
| 5 | Language | Are sentences short, clear, and free of jargon and errors? |
| 6 | Visuals | Are there screenshots or diagrams? Are they current and helpful? |
| 7 | Accuracy | Do the instructions still match the current OSM website? |
| 8 | Findability | Can a beginner easily find this page and related pages? |
| 9 | Links | Are links working and relevant? |

**Rating scale:** Good / Needs improvement / Poor
